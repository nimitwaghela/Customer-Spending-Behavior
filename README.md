# Customer-Spending-Behavior
This is a Power BI project on Customer Shopping Behavior. Dataset is from Kaggle and it has a data of about 10000 records.
Dashboard contains most of the information on where and how customer is shopping in India.
Key Insights:
  Men are shopping more than women.
  Consumer is moving towards online shopping.
  UPI is the most favorable mode of payment.
  Consumer is spending most on Clothing.
  Pune is  the city with highest number of sales.
  Puma being the most shopped brand online and offline.
  Myntra records the highest sales.
  End of Season Sale bring the most revenue in the Ecommerce business.
